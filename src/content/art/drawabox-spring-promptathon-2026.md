These are my submissions for the Spring 2026 Promptathon.
One of the big lessons I learned from this one was to not have an ego about my art- I kept saying "I can draw it better later" which is partially true, however, that was an excuse for not accepting my art at its current state. Sure, if we had a billion hours, we could produce something great. However, sometimes we ought to settle and accept what we have in front of us.
