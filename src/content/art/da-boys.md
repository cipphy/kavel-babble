This is my first digital piece using an actual drawing tablet (Huion Inspiroy H950P) and drawing software (Krita) featuring Gravel (left) and Luca (right). All previous digital drawings were done with Paint.net.
The decision to go fully digital with this was completely random- I had the tablet out for job interviews and decided to download Krita and give it a go. This was an absolute blast and I'm really happy with the perspective. I can't wait to draw more of these two!
